Files covered:
- cuckoo-ambience.wav (also bundled): quiet ambience from original seconds 2.30–2.85, mono, filtered at 100–4000 Hz, RMS normalized to -40 dBFS.
- sources/Kuckuck.ogg: original recording, retained unchanged.
- cuckoo-natural.wav: edited excerpt of the original recording.
- App bundle Resources/cuckoo.wav: byte-identical copy of cuckoo-natural.wav.
- cuckoo-natural-preview.mp3: compressed listening copy of that edited excerpt.

Changes by the 날아라뻐꾸기 project:
Extracted original seconds 1.32–2.22 (one complete two-note phrase), downmixed
to mono, applied a light 100 Hz high-pass and 4000 Hz low-pass, added an
18 ms fade-in and 80 ms fade-out, and normalized the peak to -9 dBFS.
Playback speed and pitch have not been changed. No synthetic notes added.
The MP3 preview additionally uses lossy MP3 encoding.
